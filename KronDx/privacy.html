Privacy Policy — KronDx: Neon Brick Breaker

Effective date: 2026-09-27

Summary

KronDx: Neon Brick Breaker does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app does not connect to the internet, does not show ads, does not use analytics or crash reporting, and does not require an account or sign-in. All game progress is saved only on your own device.

Data we collect

We (MiniAppFactory) do not collect any data from this app. No data is sent to us, to any server, or to any third party, because the app has no network access at all.

Data stored on your device

The app stores the following information locally on your device only, using Android's standard app-private storage (SharedPreferences). This information never leaves your device through the app:

  • In-game coin balance
  • Level progress, stars earned, and per-level high scores (up to 500 levels)
  • Owned and equipped paddle skins
  • Owned support boosters
  • Daily reward streak and the last date a daily reward was claimed
  • Sound on/off preference

None of this data identifies you personally. It is only used to remember your progress inside the game.

Permissions

The app requests no Android permissions. It does not request internet access, storage, location, camera, microphone, contacts, or any other permission.

Third-party services

KronDx: Neon Brick Breaker does not integrate any third-party SDK. Specifically, the app contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK, no billing SDK, and no login/social SDK.

The only outside parties involved are the platforms you already use to get and run the app:

  • The Google Play Store, which distributes the app and applies its own standard policies to the download and installation process.
  • Your Android device's built-in backup system. If you have Android device backup enabled on your device/Google account, the operating system may include this app's local data in that system-level backup, the same way it does for other apps. This is a feature of Android itself, not something KronDx: Neon Brick Breaker sends anywhere on its own.

Children

KronDx: Neon Brick Breaker is a general-audience neon brick-breaker arcade game and is not directed at children. It contains no chat, no user-generated content, no social features, and no real-money purchases (coins are earned and spent only inside the game). We do not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children, because the app does not collect data from anyone at all.

Your choices / deleting data

Because KronDx: Neon Brick Breaker has no account and no server-side data, there is no "delete my account" request to make. To remove all locally stored game data (coins, progress, skins, boosters, settings), do either of the following on your device:

  • Go to Android Settings → Apps → KronDx: Neon Brick Breaker → Storage & cache → Clear storage (wording may vary slightly by Android version).
  • Uninstall the app. This removes all of its locally stored data from your device.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, an updated version will be published at this same URL with a new effective date. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
