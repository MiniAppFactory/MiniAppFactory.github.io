Privacy Policy · Gizlilik Politikası — Last updated / Son güncelleme: 2026-09-27

Who we are

SEKKOI is developed by MiniAppFactory ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the SEKKOI Android game (package com.sekkoi.game) collects and how it is used. Contact: whatsthisapp@proton.me.

Who can use SEKKOI

SEKKOI is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over. It is not designed for or directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

What data is collected

SEKKOI has no account system of its own and no advertising SDK. Your level progress, coin balance, character unlocks and settings are stored locally on your device and are never sent to us.

SEKKOI uses two Google-provided services, each governed by Google's own privacy terms in addition to this policy:

ServiceWhat it does in SEKKOIData involved
Google Play Games Services Optional sign-in with your Google account to enable leaderboards. When you are signed in, your campaign total score and daily challenge score are uploaded to Google Play Games leaderboards so they can be ranked against other players. Playing SEKKOI does not require signing in, and SEKKOI does not use Play Games cloud saves. Your Google Play Games player identity and the scores you upload, associated with that account, handled under Google's Privacy Policy.
Google Play Billing Used for optional in-app coin purchases. Purchases, payment and refunds are handled entirely by Google Play. The "Restore purchases" option re-checks your Google Play purchase history and completes any purchase that was paid for but not yet delivered. Purchase and transaction data is processed by Google Play, not by SEKKOI's own servers (we do not operate one). Coin packs that were already delivered and spent cannot be restored from Google Play; your coin balance itself travels to a new phone only through Android Backup (see below).

Because Play Games Services and Play Billing are Google libraries, the device may also exchange basic diagnostic/connectivity signals with Google as part of normal Google Play Services operation, under Google's own data handling practices — SEKKOI's own code does not add tracking on top of this.

Permissions

SEKKOI requests INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE (needed for Play Games/Billing connectivity) and the Play Billing library permission. SEKKOI does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts, storage, or SMS permissions, and does not use an advertising ID.

What we don't do

  • No ads or ad networks.
  • No location tracking (precise or approximate).
  • No user-generated content, chat, or messaging between players.
  • No sale of personal data.

Data retention and deletion

Local game data (level progress, coins, unlocked characters, colors and badges, and your settings) is stored on your device and is removed when you uninstall SEKKOI or clear the app's storage. If Android Backup is turned on for your device, this same game data is included in your device's Google backup so it can be restored when you set up a new phone; that backup is managed by Android and your Google account, not by SEKKOI, and you can turn it off in your device's Google backup settings. SEKKOI does not use Play Games cloud save. Data associated with your Google account (Play Games scores, purchase history) is controlled by your Google account settings — see Google Account data & privacy to view or delete it. SEKKOI has no separate account to delete because it has no account system of its own.

Children

SEKKOI is not directed at children under 13 and is not part of Google Play's "Designed for Families" program.

Changes to this policy

If SEKKOI's data practices change (for example, when coin purchases go live or if a backend is added), this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change.

Hangi veriler toplanıyor

SEKKOI'nin kendi hesap sistemi yoktur ve reklam SDK'sı içermez. Bölüm ilerlemeniz, coin bakiyeniz, karakter açmalarınız ve ayarlarınız yalnızca cihazınızda yerel olarak saklanır, bize hiç gönderilmez.

SEKKOI, her biri Google'ın kendi gizlilik şartlarına da tabi olan iki Google servisi kullanır:

ServisSEKKOI'de ne işe yarıyorİlgili veri
Google Play Games Services Liderlik tabloları için isteğe bağlı Google hesabıyla giriş. Giriş yaptığınızda kampanya toplam skorunuz ve günlük meydan okuma skorunuz, diğer oyuncularla sıralanabilmesi için Google Play Games liderlik tablolarına yüklenir. SEKKOI'yi oynamak için giriş yapmak gerekmez; SEKKOI, Play Games bulut kaydı kullanmaz. İzinler

SEKKOI, INTERNET ve ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE izinlerini (Play Games/Billing bağlantısı için) ve Play Billing kütüphane iznini kullanır. Kamera, mikrofon, konum, rehber, depolama veya SMS izni istemez; reklam kimliği kullanmaz.

Yapmadıklarımız

  • Reklam veya reklam ağı yok.
  • Konum takibi yok (hassas veya yaklaşık).
  • Oyuncular arası kullanıcı içeriği, sohbet veya mesajlaşma yok.
  • Kişisel verinin satışı yok.

Veri saklama ve silme

Yerel oyun verisi (bölüm ilerlemesi, coin bakiyesi, açılan karakter/renk/rozetler ve ayarlarınız) cihazınızda tutulur; SEKKOI'yi kaldırdığınızda veya uygulama verisini temizlediğinizde silinir. Cihazınızda Android Yedekleme açıksa bu oyun verisi cihazınızın Google yedeğine de dahil edilir ve yeni bir telefon kurduğunuzda geri yüklenebilir; bu yedek SEKKOI tarafından değil Android ve Google hesabınız tarafından yönetilir, cihazınızın Google yedekleme ayarlarından kapatabilirsiniz. SEKKOI, Play Games bulut kaydı kullanmaz. Google hesabınızla ilişkili veriler (Play Games skorları, satın alma geçmişi) Google hesap ayarlarınızdan yönetilir — görüntülemek veya silmek için Google Hesabı veri ve gizlilik sayfasına bakın. SEKKOI'nin kendi hesap sistemi olmadığı için silinecek ayrı bir hesabı yoktur.
